# InfoSlides for Claude

InfoSlides turns presentations, photos, menus and live data into streams for the TVs and screens a business runs.
This plugin connects Claude to your InfoSlides workspace through the hosted server at https://infoslides.app/mcp, and
adds the Digital Signage Assistant skill, so you can ask Claude what is on a screen, change a slide, schedule a
menu, turn the ticker off, or undo a change. Claude asks you to sign in to InfoSlides the first time; nothing needs
to be installed.

The skill in `skills/` is generated from the InfoSlides website by `scripts/sync-skills.py` and is not edited by hand.

## Privacy Policy

InfoSlides processes presentation content, media and screen details only to render and deliver your signage, and
does not sell data or train AI models on it. Read the full policy at <https://infoslides.app/privacy>. Support:
support@infoslides.app.
