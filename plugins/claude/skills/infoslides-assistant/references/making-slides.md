# Making a slide

For "make a slide saying the lunch moved to 1pm", use `add_designed_slide` with a `heading`, optional `text` and
`cta`, a background (`backgroundColor`, a hex colour, or `mediaUrl`), and optionally a picture beside the text
(`imageUrl`) and `branding`: the words appear exactly as given, and a
text that is too long is refused with the field named rather than clipped. For a slide designed for them, use AI
Studio ([ai-studio.md](ai-studio.md)): `make_ai_slide`, show the previews to the person, change the request until
they are happy, then `insert_ai_slides`. Designing and uploading your own slide images is not possible from here.
